# QUANTFLOW - Logo Design Specification

**Premium Crypto-Inspired | Futuristic | Institutional**

## 🎯 DESIGN BRIEF

### **Core Concept**
A **quantum particle in motion** through a **hexagonal stabilizer**, with **flowing energy lines** suggesting market liquidity and algorithmic flow.

### **Inspiration Sources**
- **BTC Logo** (geometric, trustworthy)
- **Ethereum** (dynamic, scientific)
- **Stripe** (minimal, powerful)
- **Anthropic** (futuristic, balanced)

### **Key Attributes**
- ✅ Geometric (stability, precision)
- ✅ Dynamic (flow, energy, movement)
- ✅ Minimal (sophisticated, scalable)
- ✅ Tech-forward (quantum, algorithm)
- ✅ Premium (clean lines, intentional spacing)

## 🖼️ VISUAL CONCEPT

### **PRIMARY STRUCTURE**

```
The Logo consists of THREE interlocking elements:

1. HEXAGON (背景 stabilizer)
   - Regular hexagon, slightly transparent outline
   - Represents: Market stability, structure, geometry
   - Style: Thin lines (2-3px), never filled
   - Color: Cyan Electric (#00D9FF)

2. QUANTUM PARTICLE (center core)
   - Abstract curved shape (like electron orbit)
   - Represents: Algorithm, data flow, trading action
   - Style: Flowing, dynamic, asymmetric
   - Color: Solid Cyan or gradient Electric→Gold
   - Animation potential: Subtle orbit/rotation

3. ENERGY WAVES (outer accent)
   - 2-3 curved lines radiating from center
   - Represents: Market liquidity flow, information speed
   - Style: Thin, curved, suggesting motion
   - Color: Secondary accent (Purple or Teal)
   - Effect: Creates sense of kinetic energy
```

## 📐 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### **Grid & Proportions**
- **Canvas Size:** 512px × 512px (work at 2x for crisp rendering)
- **Viewbox:** 0 0 512 512
- **Safe Area:** Inner 80% (51.2px padding)
- **Stroke Width:** 3-4px at 512px scale
- **Line Cap:** Round (for premium feel)

### **Size Variants**
```
512px → Master file (all detail visible)
256px → Social media (profile pic)
128px → App icon (UI placement)
64px  → Favicon minimum
32px  → UI micro (must simplify to glyph)
```

### **Glyph-Only Version (32px+)**
When logo doesn't fit full complexity:
- Show just the **central quantum particle** with 1-2 orbit lines
- Keep signature Cyan electric color
- Should be instantly recognizable

## ✨ DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS

### **Line Weight (at 512px)**
- **Hexagon outline:** 3px
- **Particle lines:** 4px (slightly thicker for emphasis)
- **Energy waves:** 2.5px (subtle)
- **Consistency:** All lines should have same line-cap/line-join

### **Spacing & Margins**
- **Internal spacing:** Minimum 20px from hexagon to content edge
- **Clear space:** Logo should have 25px minimum margin around it
- **Alignment:** All elements centered in canvas

### **Curves & Smoothness**
- **Hexagon:** Perfect geometric hexagon (60° angles)
- **Particle:** Organic curves (Bezier, not sharp angles)
- **Waves:** Smooth sine/cosine waves (flowing, not jagged)
- **Overall:** Balance between geometric (trustworthy) and organic (dynamic)

## 🚫 DESIGN DON'Ts

❌ **Avoid:**
- Overly complex details (doesn't scale down)
- Too many colors (confusing, not premium)
- Trendy design (will age poorly)
- Skewed/rotated hexagon (looks unstable)
- Sharp angles on particle (should be organic)
- Thick strokes (looks clunky, not refined)
- Drop shadows (too Web2, not modern)
- Gradients > 2 colors (loses sophistication)

✅ **DO:**
- Keep lines clean and intentional
- Use max 2 accent colors
- Test at ALL sizes (especially small)
- Ensure 3:1 contrast ratio minimum
- Use consistent line weights
- Leave whitespace intentional
- Test on both dark + light backgrounds

## 📸 CONTEXT VISUALIZATION

### **How Logo Appears In Different Contexts:**

**Profile Picture (256px, circular mask):**
- Hexagon should be fully visible
- Waves might crop slightly (ok)
- Particle centered

**Favicon (32px):**
- Only glyph (particle + 1 wave)
- Very simplified
- Cyan only, no secondary colors

**Social Media Post (centered, dark BG):**
- 256-512px size
- Full cyan + purple/gold color combo
- Plenty of whitespace around

**Business Card (small, light BG):**
- 100px max size
- Possibly monochrome version
- High contrast required
